# ANTIGRAVITY SPEC — FIRST 7 DAYS PLAYER JOURNEY & ONBOARDING

**Version:** 1.0  
**Priority:** VERY HIGH  
**Purpose:** Design the first seven in-game days so the player learns the core store operation loop gradually, enjoys early success, experiences meaningful problems, and becomes emotionally attached to the shop.

---

# 1. ONBOARDING GOAL

The first 7 days must teach the player:

1. understand customer request
2. pick correct product
3. understand size/color
4. use fitting room
5. complete checkout
6. understand that payment creates revenue
7. restock the shop
8. understand stockout
9. experience customer reviews
10. understand cleanliness
11. hire first employee
12. understand basic shift coverage
13. see a simple business report
14. understand that good operation increases future traffic

Do not introduce:
- deep finance
- warehouse
- multiple branches
- complex transfers
- advanced marketing
too early.

---

# 2. EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLES

The first week must feel:

```text
Day 1: "Mình làm được!"
Day 2: "À, size quan trọng."
Day 3: "Khách có cảm xúc thật."
Day 4: "Một mình bắt đầu bận rồi."
Day 5: "Nhân viên thật sự giúp mình."
Day 6: "Mình phải quản lý chứ không chỉ bán."
Day 7: "Mình hiểu shop của mình hơn rồi."
```

The player should not feel overwhelmed.

---

# 3. TUTORIAL RULES

Tutorial:
- one clear task at a time
- real gameplay, not fake tutorial
- short Vietnamese instructions
- target object highlighted
- advance only after actual successful action
- allow player to retry safely
- reduce punishment during early tutorial mistakes

Avoid:
- long forced text
- unskippable lectures
- showing all systems at once

---

# 4. DAY 1 — OPENING THE FIRST SHOP

## Learning goal
Teach:
- customer request
- rack interaction
- exact item selection
- checkout
- revenue only after payment

## Opening mood
Cute morning introduction.

Example:
> "Hôm nay là ngày đầu tiên của cửa hàng!"

Shop contains only a few categories:
- T-shirt
- basic pants
- maybe simple accessory

Keep product assortment small.

## Tutorial flow

### Step 1
Customer arrives.

Dialogue:
> "Cho mình áo thun trắng size M nhé!"

Instruction:
> "Bước 1: Chạm Kệ Áo"

### Step 2
Choose style.

### Step 3
Choose white color.

### Step 4
Choose size M.

### Step 5
Place on preparation table.

### Step 6
Give item to customer.

### Step 7
Customer decides to buy.

Instruction:
> "Khách muốn thanh toán rồi!"

### Step 8
Go to POS.

### Step 9
Complete payment.

Only now:
- cash increases
- revenue increases
- cute payment animation

## Learning insight
Small note:
> "Tiền chỉ được ghi nhận khi khách thanh toán thành công."

## Day 1 difficulty
Very low.

Customer count:
small.

Patience:
generous.

## Emotional reward
- first sale celebration
- happy customer
- tiny star/reputation gain
- cute end-of-day summary

## End-of-day insight
> "Bạn đã hoàn thành ngày bán hàng đầu tiên!"

Show:
- visitors
- paid customers
- revenue
- one simple success message

---

# 5. DAY 2 — SIZE & STOCK MATTER

## Learning goal
Teach:
- exact size
- wrong size consequence
- floor stock vs backroom
- restocking

## New situation
Customer asks:

> "Cho chị áo đen size M nhé."

Sales floor:
M = 0

Backroom:
M = 3

Instruction:
> "Kệ hết size M, nhưng kho sau vẫn còn hàng."

Teach:
- open backroom
- retrieve M
- bring to customer

## Controlled mistake opportunity
Another customer requests L.

Player may intentionally be shown nearby M/L.

If wrong size selected:
- customer politely reacts:
> "Hình như mình cần size L cơ."

Patience decreases slightly.

No harsh punishment.

## Restock tutorial
After customer:
> "Kệ đang gần hết hàng rồi."

Teach:
- take stock from backroom
- refill rack

## Learning insight
> "Có hàng trong kho chưa chắc khách mua được nếu kệ không được bổ sung kịp."

## End-of-day
Introduce:
- Stockout warning
- Lost Sale concept lightly

Maybe show:
> "1 khách suýt bỏ đi vì thiếu size trên kệ."

---

# 6. DAY 3 — FITTING ROOM & CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

## Learning goal
Teach:
- fitting room
- second-size request
- patience
- satisfaction
- review

## New unlock
Fitting Room.

Customer:
> "Cho chị thử chiếc này nhé."

Flow:
- item to fitting
- wait
- customer requests another size
- player retrieves correct size
- customer becomes happy

## First patience pressure
Patience becomes more visible.

Still forgiving.

## First customer review
After checkout:
Customer leaves a 5-star review.

Example:
> "Nhân viên hỗ trợ đổi size rất nhanh, dễ thương nữa!"

Teach:
- reviews affect reputation

## Optional controlled negative case
Another customer waits a little too long.

May leave 3-star:
> "Shop đẹp nhưng mình phải chờ hơi lâu."

Explain gently:
> "Thời gian chờ có thể ảnh hưởng đánh giá."

## Learning insight
> "Trải nghiệm khách hàng không chỉ phụ thuộc sản phẩm."

---

# 7. DAY 4 — CLEANLINESS & BUSIER STORE

## Learning goal
Teach:
- store cleanliness
- small operational multitasking
- prioritization

## Traffic
Slightly higher.

## New event
Fitting room becomes dirty.

Alert:
> "Phòng thử đồ #1 cần vệ sinh."

Early-game player can clean manually.

Simple interaction:
- tap cleaning supplies
- clean fitting room

## Competing tasks
Create a light multi-task moment:

```text
Customer A waiting for size
Customer B at checkout
Fitting Room dirty
```

Do not make it impossible.

Teach:
> choose priority.

## Customer reaction
Dirty fitting room may lower satisfaction if ignored.

## Learning insight
> "Một cửa hàng sạch sẽ giúp khách thoải mái hơn."

## End-of-day
Show:
- average wait
- cleanliness score
- satisfaction

Keep explanation simple.

---

# 8. DAY 5 — HIRE FIRST EMPLOYEE

## Learning goal
Teach:
- player cannot do everything forever
- employee role
- wage
- first delegation

## Trigger
Morning message:
> "Khách đang đông hơn rồi. Có lẽ bạn cần thêm người!"

Unlock:
Recruitment.

Offer 2–3 cute candidates.

Example:

### Lan
Sales Advisor
- Service: High
- Speed: Medium
- Wage: Medium

### Minh
Sales Advisor
- Service: Medium
- Speed: High
- Wage: Lower

Player chooses.

Avoid overwhelming with 10 stats.

## Tutorial
Assign employee:
- help customers
or
- restock

Player sees employee perform real tasks.

## Teaching moment
Queue improves.

Show:
> "Có nhân viên hỗ trợ giúp bạn xử lý nhiều khách hơn."

## Wage introduction
At end of day:
Show payroll line.

Teach:
> employees help operations but increase cost.

No deep payroll system yet.

---

# 9. DAY 6 — SHIFT COVERAGE & BASIC MANAGEMENT

## Learning goal
Teach:
- traffic changes during day
- staff scheduling
- peak hours
- fatigue/overtime lightly

## New system
Simple daily shift planner.

Show forecast:

```text
Morning: Medium
Afternoon: Medium
Evening: High
```

Player assigns employee to evening peak.

## Controlled comparison
If player leaves evening under-covered:
- checkout queue grows
- patience falls

But game provides advisor hint.

Example:
> "Ca tối đang đông hơn dự kiến. Thử tăng người ở quầy nhé."

## Learning insight
> "Không chỉ số lượng nhân viên, mà đúng người đúng thời điểm cũng quan trọng."

## End-of-day
Show:
- busiest period
- average wait
- labor cost
- one simple comparison

---

# 10. DAY 7 — FIRST BUSINESS REVIEW

## Learning goal
Teach:
- business performance summary
- traffic/reputation relationship
- revenue vs simple cost
- basic planning

## Morning
Player sees:
> "Một tuần rồi! Cùng xem cửa hàng hoạt động thế nào nhé."

Introduce Weekly Review.

Keep it friendly.

## Show only core metrics

```text
Visitors
Conversion
Revenue
Basic Cost
Profit
Average Review
Best-selling Size
Most Common Stockout
```

Do not show advanced accounting.

## Explain one cause/effect

Example:

> "Khách tăng 18% vì review của bạn tốt hơn."

or:

> "Doanh thu tăng nhưng lợi nhuận chưa tăng nhiều vì bạn vừa tuyển nhân viên."

## Mini advisor
Give 2–3 suggestions:

- restock size M more often
- add evening coverage
- keep fitting room clean

## First meaningful choice
Player chooses focus for Week 2:

- improve stock
- improve service
- save money
- grow reputation

This can provide a small temporary goal/mission, not a permanent forced build.

## Emotional reward
Week 1 completion badge.

Possible:
> "Chủ Shop Tập Sự"

Cute celebration.

# 11. CUSTOMER TRAFFIC CURVE FIRST WEEK

```text

Day 2: Low
Day 3: Low–Medium
Day 4: Medium
```

# 17. FIRST WEEK FAILURE DESIGN

The player should be allowed to make mistakes.

But early failures should:
- recover quickly
- explain cause
- not bankrupt the player
- not permanently damage progression

Examples:
- wrong size
- one abandoned customer
- small negative review
- slightly poor evening shift

These are teaching opportunities.

---

# 18. FIRST WEEK ECONOMY SAFETY

Avoid early bankruptcy.

Use:
- reasonable starting cash
- small purchasing requirements
- controlled rent/payroll
- limited loss events

The player should learn cost management without feeling trapped.

---

# 19. FIRST WEEK TUTORIAL FATIGUE CONTROL

Do not tutorialize every click.

Rules:
- tutorials only for new concepts
- repeat actions without banners after 1–2 guided examples
- allow tooltips on demand
- reduce instruction density each day

By Day 7, player should perform core sales flow mostly independently.

---

Introduce recurring customers during first week.

- how to fulfill and checkout
- why money only appears after payment
- why restocking matters
- why fitting support matters
- why customer waiting matters
- why reviews matter

- the store
- first employee
- recurring customers

# 31. ONBOARDING ACCEPTANCE QUESTIONS

Antigravity must evaluate:

1. Does Day 1 produce a satisfying first sale quickly?
2. Does the player understand money recognition?
3. Is Day 2 stock lesson obvious?
4. Does Day 3 make customer experience emotionally meaningful?
5. Does Day 4 introduce multitasking without overwhelming?
6. Does Day 5 make hiring feel useful?
7. Does Day 6 teach scheduling through visible consequence?
8. Does Day 7 help the player understand business performance?
9. Is complexity introduced gradually?
10. Does the first week feel cute and fun?
11. Is tutorial text short?
12. Can the player recover from mistakes?
13. Is save progress reliable?
14. Is the player ready for Week 2 without more forced tutorial?

---

# 32. ANTIGRAVITY EXECUTION PROMPT

> Implement the first seven in-game days as a carefully staged onboarding journey, not as seven generic levels. Day 1 should teach direct fulfillment and payment; Day 2 exact size and backroom restock; Day 3 fitting, patience and reviews; Day 4 cleanliness and light multitasking; Day 5 hiring the first employee; Day 6 shift coverage and peak-hour staffing; Day 7 a friendly weekly business review. Keep the first week forgiving, cute and emotionally rewarding. Teach through visible cause and effect rather than long text. Persist tutorial progress and autosave important milestones. Gradually reveal UI and systems rather than exposing the full management game immediately. By the end of Day 7, the player should understand the basic operating logic of the store and feel attached to the shop, recurring customers and first employee.

---

# 33. FINAL EXPERIENCE TARGET

At the end of the first week, the player should say:

```text
"Mình chơi vui mà giờ mình hiểu shop vận hành thế nào hơn thật."
```

The first week must build:
- confidence
- curiosity
- emotional attachment
- business understanding
- desire to continue growing the store
